BUILD (needs a computer with Android Studio, free)
1. Unzip, open Android Studio > File > Open > select this folder. Wait for Gradle sync.
2. Connect your phone (USB debugging on) and press Run, or Build > Build APK(s)
   and copy app-debug.apk to your phone.
3. On the phone open NoReels > Open Accessibility Settings > NoReels Blocker > turn ON.
   (Android may say "Restricted setting": App info > three dots > Allow restricted settings.)
4. Open YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels - you are sent back automatically.
